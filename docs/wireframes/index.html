What’s on the page

1
HUDTerm progress, budget and income per term, approval against the 35% line, and the next election year.
2
Living island mapHand-coded SVG redrawn from the game state. Every policy adds a drawing, and pollution, drought and floods are visible.
3
Policy trayCategory filters with counts. Each card shows cost, upkeep, build time, effects and SDGs. Cards you can’t afford are locked and moved to the end.
4
End barPick up to 2, watch the running total, then end the term.
5
Live SDG scoresSix meters plus the overall average.
6
Citizen moodsFaces switch between happy, neutral and angry as the stats they care about change.
7
ProjectsWhat’s active and what’s still being built.
